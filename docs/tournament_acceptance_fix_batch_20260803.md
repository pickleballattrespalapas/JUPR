# Tournament acceptance fix batch — 2026-08-03

This staging-only batch implements the defects and usability improvements recorded during manual acceptance of Tournament Setup and Registration.

## Setup

- Sponsor ordering controls.
- Tournament days generated from the tournament date range, with fixed dates and editable labels.
- Per-day court availability and capacity controls.
- Mixed Doubles automatically uses a locked Mixed gender value.
- Dialog-based Add/Edit flows and read-only summaries for events and divisions.
- Events sorted by their earliest tournament day.
- Editable division skill values.
- Bundles that support any N eligible events.
- Clear explanations for extra variants/options.
- Visible saved-draft versus published-live status.
- Actionable links for blocked setup changes.

## Registration

- Loading feedback and persistent Registration navigation for Communications and reports.
- Event-specific partner assignment, replacement, and removal.
- Registrant extra, option, and quantity editing with inventory validation and amount recalculation.

## Acceptance posture

Automated verification does not replace manual acceptance. The related tracker records remain marked for retest until the exact staging deployment is manually reviewed.

Production systems are outside the scope of this batch.
